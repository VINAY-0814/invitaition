ADD YOUR MUSIC HERE
===================

Place your background track in this folder and name it exactly:

    wedding-theme.mp3

Path: /public/audio/wedding-theme.mp3

Notes:
- The player starts on the first tap/scroll (browsers block autoplay).
- Use the round control at the bottom-right to mute/unmute.
- Until the file exists, the control reads "Add track" and stays silent.
- Prefer a looping, ambient instrumental for the best cinematic feel.
